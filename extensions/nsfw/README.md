## The rules

**NSFW channels only.** Every command on this page works only in a channel marked as **age-restricted** (NSFW). Try it anywhere else and the bot will refuse. Discord already only lets 18+ users into those channels, so that part's on them. Server admins can flip a channel to age-restricted in its settings.

**Servers only.** None of this works in DMs.

**Content comes from other websites.** The bot just fetches it. It can't control what any of those sites return, so results can be hit or miss, and sometimes a site is simply down.

## Searching

### `sex`

The search command. On its own, `!sex` just shows its list of subcommands. Add what you want to find after the type:

```
!sex gif <what you're looking for>
!sex pics <what you're looking for>
!sex video <what you're looking for>
```

Whatever you type after the subcommand is the search.

**How the results work:**

- You get a **page-through view**. Use the buttons underneath to flip between results.
- The buttons belong to **you**, the person who ran the command. Nobody else can flip your pages.
- You can only have **one search running at a time**, and there's a short wait between searches (5 seconds for gifs and pictures, 8 seconds for videos).
- If nothing matches, the bot says so. Try different words.
- If the website is unreachable, the bot says that instead. Try again later.

| Subcommand | Other names | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| `sex gif` | none | Gifs |
| `sex pics` | `pic`, `image`, `images` | Pictures |
| `sex video` | `vid`, `pin` | Video clips (from pin.porn), with the uploader and rating shown |

## Quick FAQ

**The bot says I need an NSFW channel.**
That's the one rule. Move to an age-restricted channel, or ask an admin to mark one.

**I can't find these in the help menu.**
They're hidden. The commands are all listed on this page.

**Nothing is loading.**
The images and videos come from third-party sites, so they occasionally go down. Wait a bit and try again.

**I'm getting told to wait.**
There's a short cooldown on `n` and the `sex` search commands. A few seconds and you're good.
